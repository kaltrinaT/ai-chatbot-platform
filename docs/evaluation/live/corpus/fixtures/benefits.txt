The company covers 100% of the monthly premium for the base health insurance plan for employees.

Employees who add dependents to their health insurance plan pay 50% of the additional premium cost, with the company covering the other half.

Health insurance coverage begins on an employee's first day of employment, with no waiting period. The plan includes coverage for routine checkups, specialist visits, and prescription medications.

Employees may also enroll in a Health Savings Account (HSA), to which the company contributes $300 per year regardless of the employee's own contributions.

Employees become eligible for the 401(k) retirement plan after 90 days of employment. The company matches employee contributions dollar-for-dollar up to 4% of salary.

Employees may expense up to $500 per calendar year for professional development, including online courses, books, and conference tickets.
