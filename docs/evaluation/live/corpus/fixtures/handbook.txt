Employees receive 20 days of paid time off (PTO) per calendar year, accrued monthly.

Unused PTO of up to 5 days may be carried over into the following year; any additional unused balance is forfeited.

PTO requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least one week in advance and are subject to manager approval based on team coverage needs.

In addition to PTO, the company observes 10 paid company holidays each year, listed on the shared team calendar.

Remote work is allowed up to 3 days per week, subject to manager approval. Employees working remotely must be reachable during core hours of 10am-4pm in their local timezone.

New employees complete a 2-week onboarding program that includes a laptop setup session, benefits enrollment, and a meeting with their manager to set 30-60-90 day goals.
